How to turn your Android TV into art

You do not need a special television to have art on the wall. A Google TV or Android TV you already own can show a painting that reads as a framed print, as long as you fix the brightness, the framing and the source of the art. Here are the four steps, in the order they matter.

Step 1: dim the panel

A TV ships bright enough for a showroom. Art does not glow, it reflects light, so the biggest single change is to turn the backlight down to 25 to 35% and set the picture mode to Warm or Filmmaker Mode. Do this before anything else. A dim, warm screen already looks more like paper than a bright, blue one.

Step 2: choose art you are allowed to display

Millions of paintings are in the public domain, and several museums publish high-resolution photographs of them for anyone to use, including the Met's Open Access collection and Wikimedia Commons. That means you can show a famous painting on your own TV without a subscription. Our guide to free art for Frame-style TVs lists the sources worth using, and what resolution to aim for.

Step 3: mat and frame it, do not stretch it

Paintings are rarely 16:9. Cropping loses part of the picture and black bars look like a screen. A framer's answer is a mat: a card border with a bevelled cut edge, slightly wider at the bottom than the top. Add a frame moulding around it and the whole screen reads as one framed print. What a passe-partout is and why the bevel matters explains the detail.

Art Frame TV does this for you on Google TV and Android TV. It ships with 30 bundled images, including public-domain masterworks, and draws a bevelled mat, a frame and a soft shadow around each one. The free version includes all of that with a Dark Walnut frame or no frame.

Step 4: let it appear by itself

You can open the app from the home screen any time, so the TV becomes a picture on demand. If you want the art to appear when the TV goes idle, set it as the screensaver. On Android TV that is Settings → Device Preferences → Screen saver. Google TV hides that menu behind Ambient Mode, so it needs one extra step, covered in the setup guide.

What you will not get

A glossy panel reflects the room, and no software setting makes it matte. If the TV faces a window, move it or accept some glare. Our comparison of Samsung Frame alternatives covers that gap honestly.

Common questions

Can any Android TV show art when it is idle?
Yes. Any Google TV or Android TV running Android 8.0 or newer can run an art app, either from its icon or as the screensaver.
Is it free to turn my TV into art?
It can be. Art Frame TV's free version includes 30 bundled images, the full framing look with a Dark Walnut frame or no frame, and the screensaver. There is no subscription. Extra frames, mats and art packs are one-time purchases.
Will this look like a Samsung Frame TV?
Close, apart from the panel finish. A Frame has a matte anti-glare screen that a normal TV cannot match, but dimming the backlight and matting the image gets most of the way there.
Why does my TV look like a screen and not a picture?
Usually brightness and framing. Drop the backlight to 25 to 35 percent, use a warm picture mode, and put the image inside a mat instead of filling the screen or leaving black bars.

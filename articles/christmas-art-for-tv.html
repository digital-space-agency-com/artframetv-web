Christmas art for your TV: free paintings to show

From late November, searches for Christmas art for the TV jump several-fold. You do not need a subscription for it: many classic Nativity and winter paintings are in the public domain. Here is where to find them and how to show them well.

Updated 22 November 2026 For Google TV & Android TV

  2. Show them as framed art, not a slideshow

Classic Christmas and winter paintings

These works are old enough to be in the public domain, and high-resolution scans are published by museums and on Wikimedia Commons:

  • Adoration of the Shepherds by Gerard van Honthorst, a candlelit Nativity.
  • Hunters in the Snow by Pieter Bruegel the Elder, a winter landscape.
  • The Census at Bethlehem by Pieter Bruegel the Elder, a snowy village scene.
  • Snow at Argenteuil and The Magpie by Claude Monet, quiet snow scenes.
  • Currier & Ives winter prints from the 1800s.

Check the licence note on each source page before you download, since a museum's photograph can carry its own terms even when the painting is public domain. Our guide to free art for your TV lists the best sources.

Show them as framed art, not a slideshow

A winter painting looks best inside a mat and frame, with the TV dimmed. Turn the backlight down to about 25 to 35% and use a Warm picture mode, then follow how to turn your Android TV into art. A warm wood frame suits the candlelit scenes.

Use Google's Art gallery for no-effort Christmas art

On Google TV choose Settings → System → Ambient mode → Source → Art gallery. It rotates Google's curated art automatically, though you cannot pick specific works. Details are in art screensaver for TV.

Show your own Christmas photos too

Art Frame TV runs on Google TV and Android TV, and shows your Google Photos albums inside a mat and frame with Studio Unlock, a one-time purchase. Make a Christmas album, then put it on the TV for the season. The free version has 30 bundled images and no time limit.

Common questions

Is there free Christmas art for my TV?
Yes. Many Nativity and winter paintings are in the public domain, and Google TV's Art gallery in Ambient Mode is free.
Where can I download Christmas paintings?
Wikimedia Commons and museum open-access collections such as the Met. Check the licence note on each page.
How do I make a painting look right on a TV?
Dim the backlight to 25 to 35 percent, use a warm picture mode and put the image inside a mat and frame instead of stretching it.
Can I show my Christmas photos on Google TV?
Yes. Google Photos in Ambient Mode shows full-screen photos, and Art Frame TV can show them framed with Studio Unlock.

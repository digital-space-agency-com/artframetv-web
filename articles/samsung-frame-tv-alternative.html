Samsung Frame TV alternatives: art mode on the TV you already own

The Frame is a good product with a price to match. If what you want is a television that looks like a framed print when it is idle, you have three realistic alternatives, and one of them costs nothing. Here is how they compare, including the one thing none of them can fix.

Updated 13 November 2026 For Google TV & Android TV

  3. Option 2: a clip-on bezel

The short answer

If you already own a Google TV or Android TV, the cheapest alternative to a Frame TV is an app that frames your photos and art on the idle screen. Add a clip-on bezel if the black plastic rim bothers you. You will not match The Frame's matte anti-glare panel, which only matters in a bright room.

Option 1: an art-mode app on a Google TV or Android TV

The software side of art mode, which is most of what people notice, runs on any Google TV. An app such as Art Frame TV mounts your Google Photos or public-domain art inside a rendered frame and passe-partout mat and runs it as the screensaver. It is free, with no account and no ads.

What you give up: the brightness and motion sensor, and the matte panel.

Option 2: a clip-on bezel

Third parties sell magnetic or snap-on wooden bezels that fit popular TV sizes, typically from around $100. This changes how the TV looks when it is off as well as when it is showing art, which software cannot do. Pair it with Option 1 for the closest overall match.

Option 3: a different art-first TV

Other makers sell lifestyle TVs with art modes and matte-style panels. These close the hardware gap but cost more than a standard TV, so they only make sense if you are buying a new set anyway.

What no alternative fixes

A glossy panel reflects the room. If your TV faces a large window, no software setting changes that, and it is the one case where The Frame's panel earns its price. Everywhere else, lower the backlight to 25-35%, use a warm picture mode, and mat the image. Our guide to art mode without buying a Frame TV walks through each step.

Common questions

What is the best Samsung Frame TV alternative?
For most people, an art-mode app on a TV they already own, optionally with a clip-on bezel. It costs little or nothing, and the main thing you lose is the matte anti-glare panel.
Can I make a normal TV look like a Frame TV?
Mostly. Lower the backlight, use a warm picture mode, and display art inside a matted frame rather than full screen. A clip-on bezel completes the look when the TV is off.
Is there a free alternative to the Samsung Art Store?
Yes. Many museums publish public-domain works free to use, and an art-mode app can display them. See our guide to free art for Frame-style TVs.
Do alternatives work on Google TV?
Yes. Art Frame TV runs on Google TV and Android TV, and works as the screensaver or from the app.

TV photo frame app: show photos on your TV

A television is a large, sharp screen that is idle most of the day, which makes it a good photo frame. The catch is that a photo simply filling a TV looks like a slideshow, not a frame. Here is how to show your own photos on a Google TV or Android TV, and how to make them look like they belong on a wall.

The three ways to show photos on a TV

On a Google TV or Android TV you have three realistic options:

  • Google Photos in Ambient Mode. Built in and free, but it only shows full-screen photos and only from albums you pick in the Google Home app. See Google Photos on TV.
  • A photo frame app. An app that runs on the TV and shows your photos with its own layout, borders and timing.
  • Casting from a phone. Works for a one-off, but the phone has to stay on and connected.

What a good photo frame app does

Look for four things:

  • It runs as a screensaver, so photos appear when the TV is idle, without you opening anything.
  • It frames the photo. A mat and frame around a picture is what makes a screen read as a print. What a passe-partout is explains why.
  • It keeps your photos on the TV, with no account needed for it to work.
  • It does not charge a subscription for a wall display.

Make the photos look like prints

Whatever app you use, three TV settings matter more than the app. Turn the backlight down to about 25 to 35%, switch the picture mode to Warm or Filmmaker Mode, and avoid stretching photos to 16:9. The full list is in how to use your TV as a digital picture frame.

Set it up as the screensaver

On Android TV, pick the app under Settings → Device Preferences → Screen saver. Google TV hides third-party screensavers behind Ambient Mode, so it needs one extra step. The setup guide covers both routes.

Common questions

What is the best photo frame app for a TV?
One that runs as a screensaver, frames the photo instead of stretching it, keeps photos on the TV and does not need a subscription. Art Frame TV meets all four on Google TV and Android TV.
Can I show my own photos on Google TV?
Yes. Google Photos in Ambient Mode shows full-screen photos from albums you choose in the Google Home app. Art Frame TV can show your Google Photos and shared albums inside a mat and frame with Studio Unlock.
Do I need to leave my phone connected?
Not for an app that runs on the TV. Casting needs the phone connected, an app does not.
Will a photo frame app harm my TV screen?
Showing changing images at a lower brightness is gentler than leaving a static bright image up. Keep the backlight low and use a slideshow rather than one fixed picture.

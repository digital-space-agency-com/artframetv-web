Digital photo frame gift: use the TV they own

A dedicated digital photo frame costs money, needs wifi set-up and adds another screen. If the person you are buying for already has a Google TV or Android TV, you can give them the same thing for free or a few pounds: a shared family album that plays on the TV when it is idle, and that you can add to from your own phone.

Updated 22 November 2026 For Google TV & Android TV

Is this a good alternative to a photo frame?

It depends on the person. A TV is far larger than a 10-inch frame, and photos show with a mat and frame around them, which is the look people want. The trade-off is that the TV has to be on its idle screen, so it suits a living room more than a bedside table. If they have no Google TV or Android TV, a dedicated frame is the simpler gift.

How to set it up as a gift

  1. Create a shared album in Google Photos and add family photos.
  2. Share it with the recipient's Google account and let relatives add their own photos.
  3. On their TV, open Settings → System → Ambient mode → Source → Google Photos and pick the album. This is free and shows photos full screen.
  4. For a framed look, install Art Frame TV. Studio Unlock (one-time purchase, no subscription) shows shared albums inside a mat and frame.

Full steps are in Google Photos on your TV.

Why it works for grandparents

Once set up, nothing needs to be pressed. New photos you add from your phone show up on their TV without them doing anything, and there is no app to open or password to remember.

What to do on the day

Set it up before you wrap anything, since the recipient's TV account is needed. Print a card saying "Your TV now shows family photos when it is idle" and add a first album of photos from the past year.

Common questions

Can I use my TV as a digital photo frame?
Yes. Google TV and Android TV can show Google Photos albums when idle, and Art Frame TV adds a mat and frame around each photo.
Can family add photos from their own phones?
Yes, with a shared Google Photos album that relatives can contribute to.
Does it cost anything?
Ambient Mode with Google Photos is free. Art Frame TV's free version has 30 bundled images; showing your own albums needs Studio Unlock, a one-time purchase.
What if they do not have a Google TV?
Then a dedicated digital photo frame is the simpler gift.

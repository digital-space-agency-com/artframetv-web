Which TVs have an art mode, and is the art free?

Art mode means the TV shows artwork when idle, ideally with a matte screen and a frame. Only a few TV lines are built around it, and the art supply is not always free. Here is the short version and an alternative for everyone else.

TVs built around an art mode

Samsung The Frame is the best known, with a matte display and an Art Store. Other makers sell similar lifestyle TVs, such as Hisense and TCL art-style models. Availability and features change by year and region, so check the maker's current page before buying.

Is the art free?

It depends on the maker. Samsung includes some artwork and sells a larger catalogue as a subscription. Features differ by model and region, so confirm what your TV includes. Public-domain paintings from museums are free to use, and several apps display them.

Get the look on any Google TV

Most Google TV and Android TV sets have no art mode, but you can get close: Art Frame TV shows public-domain masterworks and your own photos in a bevelled mat and frame, with 30 artworks free. See art mode without a Frame TV and free art for your TV.

Make any TV look less like a TV

Lower the brightness, turn off motion smoothing, use a warm colour temperature and keep a dark border around the artwork. The digital picture frame guide lists the settings.

Common questions

What TVs have art mode?
Samsung The Frame is the best known. Some Hisense and TCL models offer art-style modes. Check the maker's current range.
Is Frame TV art free?
Some art is included and a larger catalogue is a paid subscription on Samsung. Public-domain art is free from other sources.
Can I get art mode on a regular Google TV?
Not as a built-in feature, but an app such as Art Frame TV shows framed artwork and works as a screensaver.

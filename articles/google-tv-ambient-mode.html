Google TV Ambient Mode: how to set it up, change it and turn it off

Ambient Mode is what Google TV shows when you stop using it. It is easy to find once you know where to look, and easy to get stuck on the wrong source. Here is how it works and how to change what it shows.

Updated 13 November 2026 For Google TV & Android TV

What Ambient Mode is

On Google TV, Ambient Mode replaces the classic screensaver. It starts after a period of inactivity and shows photos, art or other content full screen until you press a button on the remote.

Where to find it

Go to Settings → System → Ambient mode. Menu names shift a little between TV makers and software versions, so if you do not see it, check Settings → Display & Sound as well.

Choosing a source

Under Source you get Google Photos, Art gallery, and an Experimental option.

  • Google Photos shows your own albums, which you pick in the Google Home app.
  • Art gallery is Google's curated set.
  • Experimental is a fallback if the others do not work for you.

If your photos do not appear, see Google Photos on TV.

Changing or turning off the timer

In the same Ambient mode screen you can set how long the TV waits before it starts, or turn it off. A separate panel sleep timer can still switch the TV off independently, so check both if the screen goes dark sooner than you expect.

Using your own screensaver instead

Third-party apps do not appear in the Ambient mode source list on Google TV, which is why many people think their screensaver app is broken. There are two working routes: a launcher that exposes the setting, or a one-time ADB command. Both are explained in how to change the screensaver on Google TV, and the full walkthrough for Art Frame TV is in the setup guide.

Common questions

Where is Ambient Mode on Google TV?
Settings, then System, then Ambient mode. On some TVs it sits under Display and Sound.
How do I turn off Ambient Mode?
Open Ambient mode in Settings and set the timer to Off, or choose a source you are happy to see.
Why can't I choose my own screensaver app?
Google TV limits the Ambient mode source list to Google's own options. A launcher with a screensaver override or one ADB command sets a third-party screensaver.
Why are my Google Photos not showing in Ambient Mode?
Albums are chosen in the Google Home app, not on the TV. See our Google Photos on TV guide for the usual fixes.

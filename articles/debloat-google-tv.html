Debloat Google TV: remove ads and bloatware

Many Google TV owners find a home screen full of promoted rows and preinstalled apps they never asked for. You can tidy most of it without a computer, and remove the rest with one ADB command per app. Here is what is safe, what is reversible, and what to leave alone.

Updated 22 November 2026 For Google TV & Android TV

Start with the settings, no computer needed

Do these first, since they cost nothing and cannot break anything:

  • Turn off ad personalisation. Look under Settings → Privacy → Ads and opt out of ads personalisation. Menu names vary by TV maker and software version.
  • Disable preinstalled apps you never use. Go to Settings → Apps → See all apps, pick the app and choose Disable. Disabled apps stop running and can be re-enabled any time.
  • Hide the clutter. Apps you disable stop appearing in your apps row.

Some promoted rows on the home screen come from Google TV itself and cannot be switched off from settings.

Remove stubborn apps with ADB

Some preinstalled apps have no Disable button. ADB can remove them for your user account without touching the system image, so it is reversible. You need a computer on the same network.

  1. On the TV open Settings → System → About and tap Android TV OS build (or Build) seven times to unlock Developer options.
  2. In Settings → System → Developer options turn on USB debugging or Wireless debugging, depending on your TV.
  3. Connect from your computer. Pairing steps for each system are in our setup guide.
  4. List installed packages: adb shell pm list packages. Find the one you want to remove.
  5. Remove it for your user: adb shell pm uninstall -k --user 0 PACKAGE_NAME

How to undo it

Because the command only removes the app for your user, you can bring it back with adb shell cmd package install-existing PACKAGE_NAME. A factory reset also restores everything. Write down each package name before you remove it.

What not to remove

Do not remove the Google TV launcher (com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx on Google TV, com.google.android.tvlauncher on Android TV), the Play Store (com.android.vending), Google Play services (com.google.android.gms), or anything with system or settings in the name. Removing those can leave the TV without a home screen or unable to install apps. If unsure, disable instead of uninstalling.

Replace the ambient screensaver while you are at it

Since you already have ADB working, it is also the way to use a third-party screensaver on Google TV, where Ambient Mode only lists Google's own sources. See how to change the screensaver on Google TV, or install Art Frame TV for a framed art display with no ads and no account.

Common questions

Is it safe to debloat Google TV?
Disabling apps and turning off ad personalisation is safe. Removing apps with ADB for your user is reversible, but leave the launcher, Play Store and Google Play services alone.
Can I remove ads from the Google TV home screen?
Partly. You can opt out of ad personalisation and disable preinstalled apps, but some promoted rows come from Google TV itself and cannot be switched off in settings.
Will debloating void my warranty?
Removing apps for your user does not modify the system image, and a factory reset restores them. Check your TV maker's terms if you are unsure.
How do I get a removed app back?
Run adb shell cmd package install-existing with the package name, or factory reset the TV.

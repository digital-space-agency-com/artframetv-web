Google TV screensaver not working: fixes (2026)

A screensaver that never starts, or shows the wrong pictures, is usually one of four things: the setting is in a different menu than you expect, the source is not selected, the idle timer is off, or the account has no photos to show. Work through them in this order.

Updated 22 November 2026 For Google TV & Android TV

On this page

  1. Find the right menu
  2. Google Photos not showing
  3. It never starts
  4. It reverts to the default
  5. Use art instead
  6. Common questions

Find the right menu

On Google TV the setting is under Settings → System → Ambient mode. On older Android TV and Nvidia Shield it is Settings → Device Preferences → Screen saver. Menu names vary by TV maker and software version, so search the settings for "screensaver" or "ambient" if yours differs.

Google Photos not showing

In Ambient mode choose Google Photos as the source, then pick the albums to show. The albums must exist in the Google account signed in on the TV, and shared albums need to be added to that account's library first. If the TV shows the default art or clock, the selection is empty or the account has no photos available to it.

It never starts

Check the idle timer under Start time or When to start and set a short delay such as 5 minutes to test. A TV that is playing video, or an HDMI input that keeps the screen active, will not trigger the screensaver.

It reverts to the default

Some Google TV versions only offer built-in screensavers in Ambient mode. To use a third-party screensaver you can change the setting with ADB or a launcher, covered in how to change the screensaver on Google TV.

Use art instead

If you want framed art rather than a photo slideshow, Art Frame TV adds a mat and frame and works as a screensaver. See the setup guide.

Common questions

Why is my Google TV screensaver not showing my Google Photos?
Usually the Google Photos source or albums are not selected in Ambient mode, or the signed-in account has no albums available to it.
Where is the screensaver setting on Google TV?
Settings, then System, then Ambient mode. On older Android TV it is Settings, then Device Preferences, then Screen saver.
Why does my TV screensaver never start?
The idle timer may be off or very long, or an active video or HDMI input is keeping the screen awake.
Can I use a custom screensaver on Google TV?
Yes, but newer Google TV versions hide the option. It can be changed with ADB or a launcher.

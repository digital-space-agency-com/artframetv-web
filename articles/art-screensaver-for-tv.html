Art screensaver for TV: free ways to get one

Turning your TV into a rotating art gallery when it is idle is easy and can be free. There are two good routes on Google TV and Android TV, and they suit different tastes. Here is what each one gives you.

Updated 22 November 2026 For Google TV & Android TV

Route 1: Google's Art gallery

On Google TV, open Settings → System → Ambient mode → Source and choose Art gallery. Google supplies the images, they rotate automatically, and it costs nothing. The trade-offs are that you cannot choose the artworks and nothing is framed or matted. See Google TV Ambient Mode for the menu.

Route 2: a framed art app

Art Frame TV ships with 30 bundled images, including public-domain masterworks, and draws a bevelled mat, a frame and a soft shadow around each one. The free version includes all 30 images, a Dark Walnut frame or no frame, and the screensaver, with no account and no ads. More frames, mats and art packs are one-time purchases.

Where public-domain art comes from

Museums such as the Met publish high-resolution scans of works in the public domain, and Wikimedia Commons hosts many more. That is why famous paintings can be shown on a TV without a licence. Our guide to free art for your TV lists the best sources.

Make it look like art, not a screen

Dim the backlight to about 25 to 35%, use a warm picture mode and keep the painting inside a mat. How to turn your Android TV into art walks through the four steps in order.

Which should you choose?

If you want zero setup, use Art gallery. If you want a framed look, control over which images appear, or your own photos next to the paintings, use an app. You can try the app free, since the free version has no time limit.

Common questions

Is there a free art screensaver for my TV?
Yes. Google TV's Ambient Mode has a free Art gallery source, and Art Frame TV is free to install with 30 bundled images and no account.
Where do I find the Art gallery option?
On Google TV: Settings, then System, then Ambient mode, then Source, and choose Art gallery.
Can I choose which paintings appear?
Not in Google's Art gallery. An app such as Art Frame TV lets you pick from its bundled images and add art packs.
Does the art screensaver work on Android TV too?
Yes. On Android TV you pick the screensaver under Settings, Device Preferences, Screen saver.

How to put your Google Photos on your TV screensaver

Google TV can idle on your own photos instead of stock landscapes. The setup takes a minute; the part that trips people up is album selection and the handful of reasons photos silently fail to appear.

Updated 20 October 2026 For Google TV & Android TV

On this page

  1. Setting it up
  2. When photos don't show up
  3. Why it stays online-only
  4. Picking photos that actually look good on a TV
  5. Common questions

Setting it up

On the Google TV home screen go to Settings → System → Ambient mode. Choose Google Photos as the source, then sign in with the account that holds the photos. By default it will show a mix from your whole library.

To narrow it down, pick albums and select the ones you want. Creating an album specifically for the TV is worth the two minutes — it is the only reliable way to control exactly what the room sees.

When photos don't show up

Four causes account for nearly all of it.

  • Wrong account. The TV uses the account signed in to Google TV, not necessarily the one that owns the album. Check under Settings → Accounts & Sign In.
  • The album is shared, not owned. Shared albums do not always appear in the picker. Copy the photos into an album you own.
  • A TV-level setting overrides it. Several manufacturers ship their own backdrop or art mode that takes precedence. If your choices in Ambient mode have no effect, look for a separate screensaver setting in the manufacturer's menu.
  • Not enough recent photos. Some builds filter to recent items and will look empty if the album is all older uploads.

Why it stays online-only

Ambient Mode streams from Google Photos. If the TV loses its connection, the screensaver falls back to Google's own images or shows nothing. Apps that download your selection to the TV first — including Art Frame TV — keep working offline, which matters more than it sounds if your TV is on a congested network.

Picking photos that actually look good on a TV

A television is a landscape panel, so portrait photos get pillarboxed or cropped. Favour landscape shots. Avoid anything with small text. And drop the TV's backlight: photos shot in daylight look harsh at the brightness most sets ship with.

Common questions

How do I add photos to the Google TV screensaver?
Settings → System → Ambient mode → Source → Google Photos, then sign in and choose which albums to use.
Why is my Google TV screensaver not showing my Google Photos?
Usually the TV is signed in to a different Google account, the album is shared rather than owned, or the manufacturer's own backdrop setting is overriding Ambient mode.
Can I show only one album on the TV screensaver?
Yes. In Ambient mode, choose Google Photos then select specific albums. Making an album just for the TV is the most reliable way to control what appears.
Do Google Photos screensavers work offline?
No. Ambient Mode streams the images, so it needs a connection. Apps that download your chosen photos to the TV first keep working without one.

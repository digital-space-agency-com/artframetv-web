## Three findings that shaped this

1. **"passe partout" is contaminated in the US.** The 1,300/mo figure that was in the app repo's
   `store/play/listing-en-US.md` keyword table is mostly French grammar intent — the expansions are
   `passe composé`, `jouer passe compose`, and so on. Only `passe partout matting` (90/mo) is
   genuinely about framing. Keep the word in product copy; do not treat it as a volume play.
2. **"art frame for tv" (5,400/mo, KD 13) is the wrong intent.** The SERP is wall-to-wall physical
   bezel sellers — DecoTVFrames, Frame My TV, arttoframe — at $100–$299, plus Samsung and Best Buy.
   Someone searching it wants a wooden frame shipped to them, not an app. Ranking there would draw
   traffic that bounces. It was a top target in the app repo's ASO draft and has since been removed from it.
3. **AI Overviews appear on 4 of the 6 target SERPs.** Answer-shaped content wins those, so every
   article leads each section with the direct answer and carries `FAQPage` schema.

## On-page

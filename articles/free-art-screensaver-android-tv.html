Free art screensaver for Android TV and Google TV

A good art screensaver should not need an account, a subscription or an ad break. Art Frame TV is free to install and use, with optional one-time purchases for more frames, mats and art. This page is plain about which is which.

What is free

The free version of Art Frame TV includes:

  • All 30 bundled images, including public-domain masterworks
  • The full framing look: a bevelled mat, depth shadow and glazing
  • A Dark Walnut frame, or no frame
  • The screensaver, with no time limit

There is no account to create and there are no ads.

What costs extra

Studio Unlock is a one-time purchase. It adds your own Google Photos and shared albums, all six mouldings, all six mat colours and finer control of the look. Frame packs and art packs are separate one-time purchases. Nothing renews, and nothing is required to keep using the free version.

Where the art comes from

The bundled artwork is in the public domain. The works are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and the Met's Open Access collection, so there is no copyright issue in showing them on your wall. Read more in our guide to free art for your TV.

How to switch it on as a screensaver

Install Art Frame TV from Google Play on the TV. On Android TV, pick it under Settings → Device Preferences → Screen saver. On Google TV, Google's Ambient Mode does not list third-party apps, so you set it once with a launcher that has a screensaver override, or with one ADB command. The step-by-step setup guide covers both, and you can always just open the app from its icon.

Privacy

The app has no account system and no analytics SDKs, and your photos stay on the TV. The privacy policy has the details.

Common questions

Is there a free art screensaver for Android TV?
Yes. Art Frame TV is free to install and includes 30 bundled images, framing and the screensaver, with no account and no ads.
Does the free version have a time limit?
No. The free version has no time limit and no trial period. The paid options are optional, one-time purchases.
Is there a subscription?
No. Studio Unlock, frame packs and art packs are one-time purchases, and nothing renews.
Can I use it as the screensaver on Google TV?
Yes, with one extra step, because Google TV's Ambient Mode only lists Google's own sources. See the setup guide for the launcher and ADB routes.

Delete your data

This page is about Art Frame TV, the Google TV and Android TV app published by Digital Space Agency UG. It sets out exactly how to delete what the app stores, what is removed, what is kept, and for how long.

Applies to Art Frame TV (team.dsa.artframetv) Last updated 4 November 2026

The short version

Art Frame TV has no user accounts, so there is no account to close and no profile to erase. Everything the app keeps is stored on your own television, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it. We hold nothing about you on our side that outlives a sign-in by more than fifteen minutes.

How to delete your data

1. Remove everything from the television

On the TV, go to Settings → Apps → Art Frame TV → Clear data, or simply uninstall the app. Either one deletes, immediately and permanently:

  • the photos you imported from Google Photos or from your phone;
  • the stored Google OAuth access token, if one is present;
  • your frame, mat and slideshow settings.

2. Revoke the app's access to Google Photos

Independently of the app, you can withdraw the permission you granted. Open myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Art Frame TV, and remove its access. This is a change in your Google account rather than in the app, so do it even if you have already uninstalled.

3. Ask us directly

Email info@dsa.team with the subject Data deletion request. You do not need to identify yourself with an account, because there is no account — tell us what you would like deleted and we will act on it and reply within 30 days. In almost every case the honest answer is that we hold nothing about you, and we will confirm that in writing.

You can also write to us: Digital Space Agency UG, Eilenau 18, 22087 Hamburg, Germany.

What is deleted, what is kept

DataWhere it livesDeleted when
Photos you imported Your television, in the app's private storage You clear the app's data, replace the selection, or uninstall
Google OAuth access token Your television, in the app's private storage You clear the app's data or uninstall; the token also expires on its own
Frame and slideshow settings Your television You clear the app's data or uninstall
Sign-in hand-off document Our Firebase project, briefly, during phone sign-in only Automatically, when the hand-off completes or after 15 minutes

Retention

There is no additional retention period and no backup copy to wait out. Data on the television is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer, so clearing it on the device clears it everywhere. The sign-in hand-off document is the only thing that ever reaches our infrastructure, and it is deleted when the hand-off finishes or expires after fifteen minutes, whichever comes first. It contains a short-lived access token and a picker URL — no name, no email address, no photograph and no identifier we could tie back to you.

We keep no server-side record of who installed the app, so after a deletion there is nothing left for us to find. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy; we never see your payment details and cannot delete your Play purchase history.

The full detail behind this page is in our Privacy Policy — see §12, Deleting your data, and §6 for the sign-in hand-off.
